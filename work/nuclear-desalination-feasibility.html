Released

Small and micro modular reactors coupled with reverse-osmosis desalination in NSW — one systems-level feasibility assessment.

Objective

Align energy supply, water demand, site infrastructure, regulation, cost and risk for an integrated energy-and-water system, rather than compare single technologies.

Approach

  1. 01Defined the coupled system boundary across reactor, electricity supply, desalination plant and supporting infrastructure.
  2. 02Compared small modular reactor and micro modular reactor concepts as energy sources for reverse osmosis.
  3. 03Assessed New South Wales site selection considerations and supporting infrastructure.
  4. 04Integrated regulatory, economic, water-security and risk factors into the feasibility assessment.

Outcomes

  • ✓A structured feasibility assessment for nuclear-powered reverse-osmosis desalination in NSW.
  • ✓Demonstrated analysis of a complex infrastructure proposal across several engineering and policy constraints.
