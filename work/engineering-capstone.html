Objective

Answer the client's research question — how to put out a small fire in the middle of nowhere — with a physical, working suppression device for quarter-hectare remote bushfires, carried through a full Vee-model systems process.

Approach

  1. 01Vee-model process for a five-member ANU capstone team: ConOps → 11 pairwise-weighted needs → 23 requirements traced to client minutes → House of Quality across 16 design criteria → technical performance measures.
  2. 02Two-stage retardant evaluation across water, salts and commercial retardants selected hydromagnesite — non-toxic, water-soluble, endothermically decomposing; Phos-Chek types and KCl failed the environmental and toxicity gates.
  3. 03Seven concepts generated; pairwise and 11-criterion screening selected the pogo-stick water-dispersal device — zero minus scores on safe release, material compliance, delivery area and payload dispersion.
  4. 04Prototyped in flexible biodegradable PLA: four pellet iterations and two propeller iterations merged into the final seven-component design; three test iterations against six selected TPMs.
  5. 05Measured with the USDA cup-and-grid drop test (the airtanker evaluation standard, supplied by CSIRO): velocity improved 9.57 → 10.38 → 12.38 m/s across iterations; 72 g of added mass matched 120 g performance.
  6. 06Owned the systems-engineering process end to end — decision log, risk register, repository and naming conventions — with four assessment audits closed through a live feedback register.

Outcomes

  • ✓All six selected TPMs met or exceeded: 12.38 m/s drop velocity, >2 kg/s dispersion rate, 0.5 kg total weight, and 77 g of a 93 g load delivered to the central target zone.
  • ✓A physical, working, single-use misting device — a tangible output earlier iterations of the capstone had not achieved — delivered with CAD files, a user manual and a handover plan.
  • ✓Modelling showed the quarter-hectare extinguishing goal reachable within four ANU waterglider drops at 500 kg each.
  • ✓High Distinction for the individual capstone component; ENGN4300 overall: 78 (Distinction).

Note on record

Five-member ENGN4300 capstone for ANU ICEDS. Validated against controlled drop tests and modelling — no live-fire testing was conducted; suppressant volume was validated by scaling argument. The full evidence archive (195+ documents, test data and video) is held privately.
