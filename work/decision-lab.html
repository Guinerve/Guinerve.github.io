One lab. Eleven decisions worth examining.

The underlying site offers guided walkthroughs, experiment controls and state inspectors. This portfolio preview illustrates three decision boundaries; the lab's five selected studies and six exploratory studies are catalogued below.

Objective

Show where a model may help interpret operational state and where tested interlocks and human operators retain authority.

Approach

  1. 01Built five featured case studies and six exploratory studies, each with a guided walkthrough, controllable experiment and state inspector.
  2. 02Grid restoration and warehouse autonomy use tested deterministic models; the warehouse also has a 3D floor with a 2D fallback.
  3. 03Jev receives an explicit observation and returns an advisory assessment. It cannot actuate the simulations or physical equipment.
  4. 04Compared grid restoration against a fixed-sequence baseline on the same model inputs and clock; the comparison is a model counterexample, not a measured utility result.

Outcomes

  • ✓A navigable set of interactive studies with shared scenario state across walkthrough, experiment and inspector modes.
  • ✓Grid and warehouse model invariants are tested; other cases remain illustrative studies.

Note on record

The language-model interpretation layer is proposed, not implemented. Jev responses are advisory; reference traces appear when a TypeSafe key is absent. Scenarios are deterministic demonstrations, not field benchmarks.
